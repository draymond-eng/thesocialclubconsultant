The story

I left finance to build Three Cities Social.

What most adults are missing isn't ambition. It's connection.

On leaving Merrill Lynch

I built Three Cities Social in River North around one idea: make it easy to see the same people again. It worked. The club grew from 25 founding members to 250+. It runs 30-45 events a month.

The timeline.

  1. July 2024

    Opened in River North with 25 founding members.

  2. May 2025

    250+ members.

  3. Today

    250+ members and 30-45 events a month.

Knowing when to close.

I opened a second location and closed it on purpose. The numbers said walk, so I walked away from a ~$1.4M lease renewal. Most consultants only sell growth. I'll help you decide when to expand and when to stop.

Who it is for

I help people build clubs that work.

I signed the leases, set the prices, ran 500+ events, made payroll, and closed the location whose numbers didn't work. You get what I learned doing it.

  1. 01

    The aspiring founder

    You have an idea and maybe a waitlist. Prove people want it before you sign a lease. Sign up founding members before you open. Check the numbers before you look at spaces.

  2. 02

    The organizer

    Your run club, dinner series, or league already draws a crowd. You want to turn that into paid membership and keep what people like about it.

  3. 03

    The space

    You run a venue, studio, or coworking floor with empty hours. A membership can fill them if you build it as a club, not a discount.

  4. 04

    The brand or company

    You want members who come back. Not another one-off event that ends when the budget does.

What I believe

The Ten Laws

Ten rules the club runs on. I learned each one the hard way before I wrote it down. Here is the first. The other nine are for clients and the Circle.

Law I

Charge to show up.

People don't value what costs nothing. Add even a small, refundable deposit and people show up.

Proof: Our deposit-backed volleyball league never forfeited once.

Laws II through X

Pricing, events, culture, growth, and money. Each one comes with the story behind it. Clients and the Circle only.
