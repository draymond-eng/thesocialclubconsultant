Who it is for

Founders, brands, organizers, and spaces.

The work is for people starting a club, and for people who already have a crowd, a brand, or a room.

  1. 01

    Founders

    You have the idea, and maybe a list of people who said they would come. The work is the order of operations: proof before a lease, a founding group before an opening, and a hard look at the numbers before a tour with a broker.

  2. 02

    Brands

    You want members, rituals, and people who return. Not an activation that ends when the budget does.

  3. 03

    Organizers

    A run club, a dinner series, or a league already draws a crowd. You want membership, and revenue, without losing the reason people show up.

  4. 04

    Spaces

    You operate a venue, a studio, or a floor with quiet hours. Membership can be the strongest margin in the building when it is a club, and not a discount.

About

What most adults are missing isn't ambition. It's connection.

On leaving Merrill Lynch

I built Three Cities Social in River North so it would be easy to see the same people again. The club grew from 25 founding members to 250+. It runs 30-45 events a month and took no venture capital.

  1. October 2022

    A free events group. No lease and no dues. Proof that people would keep showing up.

  2. July 2024

    Opened in River North with 25 founding members.

  3. Today

    250+ members, 30-45 events a month, and no venture capital.

Knowing when to close is part of the job.

I opened a second location, and I closed it on purpose. When the numbers said walk, I walked away from a ~$1.4M lease renewal, and the club is stronger for it. Most consultants only sell the growth story. I'll help you know when to expand, and when to stop.
